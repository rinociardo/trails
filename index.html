Settings

Alerts

Straight segments of a planned track have no path to follow, so you may stray further from the line there.

Battery

Dim shows a black screen, which uses very little power on the iPhone's OLED display. The map stops updating while dimmed.

GPS smoothing

More fixes give a steadier position but it trails behind you: about half a second per fix (5 fixes ≈ 2 s ≈ 3 m at walking pace).

Stop detection

The distance grows to the GPS accuracy when that is worse. The climb check stops slow, steep climbs from counting as stops: 3 m in 30 s is a climbing rate of 360 m/h. Lower it if slow climbs still show "Stopped"; it needs a track with elevations. The debug view's "Climb" line shows the value over the last window, to help you choose.

Elevation

Removes small ups and downs from total ascent/descent. Compare with Maps 3D PRO on the same GPX to tune it.

Testing

Simulated GPS walks the loaded track at 10× speed with GPS noise, a 45 s stop and a 120 m detour. With no track loaded it walks a 2 km square from your last recorded point (or the map centre), to test free recording. The app reloads when you switch it. Remember to turn it off before a real hike.

Track

"Test loop here" draws a square loop of about 800 m starting where you stand, for a walk-around test. It is not kept when you close the app, unless a hike with it is in progress.

Tracks without elevations (free recordings, GPX files without them, the test loop) get them from a DEM (Digital Elevation Model) when online; the panel says how many points have them and from where. "Fill elevations" retries after being offline.

Saved walks

None yet. After a recording, tap "Save as track" (in the panel, or below) to keep the walk as a track you can load and follow.

"New track" plans a route on the map; "Edit" changes a saved one. Editing a recorded walk saves a new track and keeps the recording.

Planning

Trail segments follow footpaths using openrouteservice (foot-hiking), through the Trails relay: nothing to set up. When routing is unavailable (offline) or its daily limit is reached, segments are drawn straight.

Slopes are shown in degrees and as a grade: 15° = 27%, 20° = 36%, 30° = 58%, 45° = 100%.

Export (GPX)

"Export track" saves the loaded route with its elevations. "Export my hike" saves the path you actually walked, with the time of each point (one segment per Start/Stop; elevations come from the route where you were on it; a free recording, made with no track loaded, has none and says so in the file). On iPhone and in Safari on the Mac the Share sheet opens: Save to Files, AirDrop, Messages. Other browsers download the file.
